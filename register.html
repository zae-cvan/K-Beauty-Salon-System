    Terms & Conditions

    Effective date: August 12, 2026 · K-Beauty Salon & Aesthetic, San Pablo City, Laguna

    1. Acceptance of Terms

    By creating an account, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and our privacy practices. If you do not agree, please do not register or use the client portal.

    2. Personal Information We Collect

    When you register, we collect information you provide directly, including:

    • Account details: full name, email address, and password (stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
    • Personal details: phone number, date of birth, and gender
    • Beauty profile: skin type, hair type, allergies, sensitivities, and stylist notes
    • Optional profile photo, if you choose to upload one
    • Booking history, appointment preferences, and feedback you submit after visits

    3. How We Use Your Information

    Your information is used to:

    • Create and manage your client account
    • Process appointment bookings, reminders, and salon communications
    • Personalize service recommendations on your dashboard
    • Inform stylists of allergies, sensitivities, and treatment preferences for your safety
    • Improve our services, subject to your notification preferences

    4. Data Storage & Security

    Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage). We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; you share information at your own risk.

    5. Sharing of Information

    We do not sell your personal information. Your profile and booking details may be accessed by authorized salon staff and administrators solely to provide services, manage appointments, and operate the business. We may disclose information if required by law.

    6. Your Rights & Choices

    You may update your profile information at any time in the client portal. You may opt in or out of promotional emails via your notification settings. You may request account deletion through the profile page; upon deletion, we will remove or anonymize your data except where retention is required by law or legitimate business needs.

    7. Age Requirement

    You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must create and manage the account on your behalf.

    Bookings are subject to salon availability and staff confirmation. Cancellation requests submitted through the portal are reviewed by salon administration. Repeated no-shows or late cancellations may affect future booking privileges.

    10. Marketing Communications

    Appointment reminders and booking confirmations are sent by default. Promotional updates are optional and can be disabled in your profile settings at any time.
